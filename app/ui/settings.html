Themed apps

Everything is on by default. Switch an app off to keep its own look: Palette takes its line out of that app's config (backed up first) and restores it.

Get themes

Community themes made for Omarchy; they work here too, wallpapers included. The list is built in, so browsing stays offline; Install downloads that one theme. Palette takes only its colours, wallpapers and preview, never config files or scripts.

Wallpapers

Each theme has its own wallpapers. Switching to a theme shows the starred one (☆ on hover), or its first; a theme with none leaves your desktop alone.

Move to another Mac

Export makes a plain folder with your themes and settings (and, if you like, wallpapers and app configs). Copy it over with AirDrop, a USB stick or a private git repo, then Import it on the other Mac.

Bar

Your SketchyBar, built from pills. Drag items to reorder them or move them between pills, click one to change it, and press Apply. Colours follow the theme.

Windows

AeroSpace's gaps, layout, workspaces, app rules and keyboard shortcuts. Palette changes these key by key in your aerospace.toml; comments and everything else stay as you wrote them.

Borders

The coloured outline JankyBorders draws around windows. Changes show straight away.

Prompt

Your Starship prompt, built from coloured blocks. Palette changes your starship.toml key by key; other settings and comments stay. Colours follow the theme.

Dock & menu bar

macOS's own settings, the same switches as in System Settings. They change straight away, and switching back is the same switch.

Schedule
